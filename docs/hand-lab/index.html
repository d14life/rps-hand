What moves, and what am I editing?

Each finger has three movable joints. The four fingers use MCP (knuckle), PIP (middle), DIP (tip). The thumb uses CMC (base / opposition), MCP, IP. The current driver can rotate each joint in three dimensions; it does not apply anatomical limits. This lab exposes all 45 finger rotation values.

X bends / straightens, Y moves sideways, and Z twists around the finger segment. The colored arrows show the exact local axes for the selected joint. The thumb has its own angled frame. These are model editing axes, not measured medical joint angles. Try a small positive or negative change to see its direction. Joint positions and segment lengths stay fixed.

The wrist follows the live palm orientation. Saved poses change only finger rotations, so recognizing a fist does not freeze the wrist. Limits are off until you enable them yourself. Saved examples and limits stay in this browser; export JSON to back them up or move them to another PC.
